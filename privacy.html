Privacy Policy

Free Busy Viewer · Effective October 18, 2026

Summary

Free Busy Viewer does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. It has no server. It runs entirely in your Chrome browser and communicates only with Google.

Data accessed from Google

  • Free/busy times (start and end of busy periods) of calendars that their owners have shared with you. Shown to you in the extension, then discarded. Never stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Calendar sharing settings of your own primary calendar, only if you use the "share my free/busy" feature. Used to list, add or remove "free/busy only" access for the email addresses you enter.
  • Your Google account email address, read from Chrome, to show which account you are signed in with.

Event titles, descriptions, locations, attendees and other event details are never requested or accessed.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves the email address of the calendar you chose to view and your display preferences (days ahead, working hours) in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. Removing the extension deletes them.

Sharing

No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. Google sign-in tokens are managed by Chrome and used only to call Google Calendar APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Free Busy Viewer's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • Calendar owners can stop sharing at any time in the extension or in Google Calendar settings.
  • Anyone can revoke the extension's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
