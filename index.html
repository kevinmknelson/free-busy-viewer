Free Busy Viewer

Free Busy Viewer is a Google Chrome extension for small teams. It lets team members see when a colleague is busy or free, after that colleague has chosen to share their calendar's free/busy information with them.

How it works

  • The calendar owner uses the extension to give specific people "See only free/busy" access to their Google Calendar. This is a standard Google Calendar sharing setting that can be removed at any time.
  • Each team member signs in with their own Google account. The extension asks Google for the owner's busy times and shows the open time slots.
  • Event titles, descriptions, locations and guests are never accessed or shown.

Google permissions used

  • calendar.freebusy: to read the busy times of calendars that were shared with you.
  • calendar.acls: only when the calendar owner uses the sharing feature, to add or remove "free/busy only" access for the people they choose.

The extension has no server. Everything runs in your browser and talks directly to Google. See the Privacy Policy.
